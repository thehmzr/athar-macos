## Qur'anic text

Verses are the **Uthmani** text, retrieved from the [quran.com](https://quran.com)
API and byte-identical to that source.

English renderings are the **Saheeh International** translation, also from
quran.com, with footnote markers removed. The translation remains the property
of its publisher and is included here for reference alongside the Arabic.

## Hadith

Arabic texts are taken from the vocalised (*mushakkala*) editions of Sahih
al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan al-Tirmidhi, Sunan Ibn Majah and Sunan Abu
Dawud. Each entry carries its collection as its attribution. English renderings
of the hadith were written for this application.

## Other quotations

Arabic literary and proverbial quotations, and the English set, were compiled
and attributed by hand. They are drawn from works long in the public domain.
